# NRTidalv2 Pv2 aligned BNS carrier before rotation

## Scope and status

Specification **`nrtidalv2-pv2-aligned-carrier/v1`** is a conditional,
positive-frequency composition gate. It accepts a supplied nonzero,
face-on IMRPhenomD BBH carrier with its phase and time origin already set.
It adds the published finite-size correction before Pv2 rotation. It does
not select BNS precession angles or qualify a complete Pv2 BNS waveform.

## Inputs and conventions

Inputs are positive GW frequencies in Hz; component masses in solar masses;
dimensionless aligned spins and positive tidal deformabilities; luminosity
distance in Mpc; and a complex face-on BBH plus-strain carrier at each
frequency. CPU FP64 and positive, nonzero BBH carrier on all live bins are
the tested domain. The supplied BBH carrier uses `h=A exp(-i psi)` with
face-on plus-strain normalization. No reference-frequency, phase, or time
alignment is applied to the finite-size correction. The tested variant is
the published Pv2 composition, distinct from paper-D and the historical
`binary_compatible` D default.

## Mathematics and components

[Dietrich et al. (2019), Eqs. (3)–(4), (17)–(25), (26)–(29), Table II and
Sec. III.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.06011) prescribe the complex
aligned carrier, before precession rotation, as

\[
C_{\rm BNS}=W(f)\left[C_{\rm BBH}
 + A_T(f)\frac{C_{\rm BBH}}{|C_{\rm BBH}|}\right]
\exp\{-i[\psi_T(f)+S_2(f)+S_3(f)+S_{3.5}(f)]\}.
\]

`A_T` is the **signed** face-on plus-strain amplitude of Eq. (24), with
luminosity distance as traced in the [mode-normalization
specification](nrtidalv2-pv2-tidal-mode-normalization.md). `W` is the
published merger-to-`1.2 f_mrg` Planck taper using the cited 2018 merger
fit. Eq. (28) fixes the `C_Q` fit. The mode to be rotated is
`2 C_BNS / sqrt(5/(4 pi))`, as already independently gated. The phase is
added to the BBH phase; the signed amplitude is added before taper. No
BBH-amplitude ratio, amplitude refit, or binary-derived affine correction
enters the formula.

## Acceptance and limitations

The three frozen spinning systems of the [aligned-additive-term
specification](nrtidalv2-pv2-aligned-additive-terms.md) use 100 Mpc,
coalescence phasing, and bins at 100 Hz, 800 Hz, and fractions
`0.75, 0.99, 1.0, 1.025, 1.05, 1.10, 1.15, 1.25` of the *printed-fit*
merger frequency. The BBH carrier is generated by native IMRPhenomD and
then passed unchanged to the independent 80-digit reference. Each native
complex carrier must agree to `1e-10` pointwise relative to the nonzero
`|C_BBH| W + |A_T| W` scale. The strictly post-taper bin must be exact
complex zero. No phase/time alignment or output rescaling is allowed.
At supplied nonzero rotation angles, the positive-22 projection of the
assembled carrier has the same `1e-10` scale limit against the scalar
projector. This test is conditional on the separately qualified BBH
carrier and supplied rotation angles; it is not an independent test of
their generation.

The 2019 paper specifies insertion before rotation, but does not by itself
fix the native BNS angle dynamics, remnant and spline-time conventions, or
the reference-frequency anchoring needed to wire this helper into the
public Pv2 driver. The published Love–Q Eq. (28) versus Fig. 7 and merger
fit versus pinned-binary conflicts remain open. Zero or negative Lambda,
zero live BBH amplitude, and binary parity are outside this gate.
