# Standalone waveform family: provisional research ledger

This is a research specification, **not** a catalog definition or a whole-model verification claim. It records six non-SEOBNR standalone classes as found at foundation commit `5bc907d` (2026-09-24). Five remain public; the complete `TEOBResumS` waveform has since been [retired from releases](../waveforms/research/retired-release-surface.md), while scoped TEOB equation components remain. Its descriptions below are a historical code audit, not current usage instructions. The IDs are proposed component identities, not registered catalog IDs. Source locations and carrier behavior are deliberately separate. `pending` means the equation or convention has not been resolved; `conditional` means declared input or assets are required; `executable` means a stated independent scalar expression exists; `diagnostic` means a check or prototype that does not establish production mathematics. The CW quadrupole amplitude, conditional Kerr 220 carrier, angular projection, selected QNM inputs, dominant-22 memory, and leading EMRI Peters flux now have independent scalar references; the other proposed components remain outside `diffgw_reference`.

## Inputs, conventions, and options

### KerrRingdown

`times` are seconds, `frequencies` Hz, `final_mass` solar masses, dimensionless `final_spin`, `t0` seconds, and sky angles radians. The frequency transform uses `∫h(t)e^{-2πift}dt`; output is a real TD pair or complex FD pair. Each supplied positive-`m` `(l,m,n)` coefficient has an equatorial-symmetry mirror. The default angular basis is spherical; `spheroidal` evaluates the spin-weighted angular Teukolsky function. Cook–Zalutskiy [Eqs. (47), (48b–c), (49)](https://arxiv.org/html/1410.7698v2) fix the angular phase and mirror reflection: both projected terms carry `exp(+imφ)`, and the spheroidal projection reduces to the spherical one at zero spin. The default QNM backend is `independent`; an optional Berti fit exists. `energy_fraction` and `amplitudes` exclude each other; physical-energy normalization additionally needs distance in Mpc and the spheroidal basis. Without `energy_fraction`, unit amplitude is merely a coefficient, not a distance-calibrated strain. The mirror energy convention and onset derivative exclude the artificial step discontinuity. The generated QNM data asset is [`kerr_qnm_generated.py`](https://github.com/xangma/diffgw/blob/5bc907d/src/torchwave/approximants/kerr_qnm_generated.py), SHA-256 `82da71c19c50defeab4c32b6950141d845214909e9f52342e3f1cf3b46f08326`, generated in-repo from Kerr eigenvalue equations under MIT terms; it is not a downloaded Cook table. The JAX angular eigensolver includes CPU callback paths, so transform/derivative coverage of every spheroidal option remains a gate.

### TEOBResumS

`f` is Hz; component masses are solar masses, aligned `chi1/chi2` dimensionless, `lambda1/lambda2` dimensionless tidal deformabilities, `cq1/cq2` spin quadrupole coefficients, distance Mpc, angles radians, `tc` seconds. Outputs are complex FD polarizations. Default `dt_geom=0.5`, `num_steps=1500`, `integration_mode="diagnostic_fixed"`, `matching_method="diagnostic_fixed"`; this duration is **not** a physical merger time. `physical_event` finds an orbital-frequency peak and uses a piecewise-linear Fourier integral, with different Torch and JAX peak localization. The Torch-only clean nonspinning vacuum route uses continuous attachment; its NQC spline and ringdown pieces must be qualified as a distinct variant. JAX accepts the matching/integration arguments but has no clean-route dispatch. `f_lower` defaults to the smallest positive requested frequency. Tidal fallback applies a heuristic merger taper (`taper_width_ratio=0.1`), not a calibrated postmerger. The source-to-code identity of several numerical fit coefficients remains to be audited; no external author fit asset is declared by this prototype.

### ContinuousWave

`t` and `t0` are GPS seconds; `f0` is **GW** Hz (twice rotation frequency), `f1` Hz/s, `f2` Hz/s², right ascension/declination and polarization/initial phase radians, `cos_iota` dimensionless. `h0` is dimensionless strain; otherwise `ellipticity`, distance in **meters**, and default `Izz=10^38 kg m²` construct it. Output is detector strain, not a polarization pair. The orbit is a circular 1-AU approximation plus diurnal position, with no ephemeris, Einstein/Shapiro or binary timing correction. Both class wrappers now pass a nondefault `i_zz` to the ellipticity amplitude path. Time/phase origin and antenna conventions need direct detector-level verification.

### MemoryWaveform

`times` are seconds. Inputs are a dictionary of oscillatory complex modes or supplied `h_plus,h_cross`; the class wrapper accepts the latter only. A supplied `distance` is guessed to be Mpc below `10^15` and meters otherwise. If omitted, a small flux estimate triggers an assumed 100-Mpc scale. `scale_factor` is an unconstrained multiplier. These branches make physical units ambiguous. The helper uses a first-sample time spacing for derivatives and cumulative trapezoidal integration, and sets memory to zero at the first sample. `h_cross^mem=0` and the angular pattern is `sin² i`; this is an `l=2,m=0` truncation, not the full angular dependence. Torch uses `.item()` for branch decisions, limiting traced/compiled and derivative behavior. A reusable independent reference must require an explicit unit system and distance rather than reproducing the heuristic.

The new `evaluate_22(times, h22, distance_m, inclination)` path in both classes
does require an explicit distance in meters. It assumes the symmetric negative-m
mirror and uses the [published dominant-pair formula](memory-dominant-22.md),
including the `l=4,m=0` part of the full plus pattern. This method has no
`scale_factor` calibration option. The older `__call__`/`forward` path remains
a diagnostic proxy with the branch behavior described above.

### EMRIKludge

The central and secondary masses are solar masses, `p0` in central-mass units, eccentricity dimensionless, spin dimensionless, angles radians, `t` seconds and distance Mpc. `n_harmonics=5` by default. The trajectory uses *initial* fluxes to form linear `p(t),e(t)` and clamps them; phases are rectangular cumulative sums on the first time spacing. The `e` argument to `kerr_geodesic_frequencies` does not affect its frequencies. The computed nodal phase is not included in the emitted `n φ+2γ` harmonic phase. `project_to_lisa` multiplies the polarizations by static `A/E/T` antenna patterns; that alone is not a demonstrated time-delay-interferometry transfer function. Differentiability at clamps and long-time validity are unresolved.

### ParameterizedPostEinstein

`f` is Hz, masses solar masses, distance Mpc, `lambda_g` meters, redshift dimensionless, `beta_ppe` dimensionless in the **carrier's** current variable, `b_ppe` an exponent (default `-2`). The default carrier is IMRPhenomD, with other callable FD carriers accepted; source-level phase deformation is only as well qualified as the selected carrier. The helper clamps nonpositive frequencies to `10^-15` Hz; its physical domain should be positive frequency. It multiplies *both* GR polarizations by `exp[-i(δΨ_ppE+δΨ_disp)]`. `lambda_g=∞` zeros the dispersion term. The Torch dispersion helper's Python conditional over a tensor constrains batched/traced use; JAX uses `where`. The paper's amplitude deformation and merger/ringdown ppE branches are absent.

## Implementation mapping

The native files are [`torchwave/approximants`](https://github.com/xangma/diffgw/blob/5bc907d/src/torchwave/approximants/) and [`jaxwave/approximants`](https://github.com/xangma/diffgw/blob/5bc907d/src/jaxwave/approximants/), with matching basenames `kerr_ringdown.py`, `teobresums.py`, `continuous_wave.py`, `memory.py`, `emri_kludge.py`, and `ppe.py`. Kerr energy has an additional Torch file `kerr_ringdown_energy.py`; JAX defines its counterpart inside `kerr_ringdown.py`. The independent CW scalar references are `src/diffgw_reference/continuous_wave_quadrupole.py` and `src/diffgw_reference/continuous_wave_phase_response.py`; the Kerr 220 carrier, angular projection, and selected QNM input references are `src/diffgw_reference/kerr_ringdown.py`, `src/diffgw_reference/kerr_angular.py`, and `src/diffgw_reference/kerr_qnm.py`. The scoped EMRI flux reference is `src/diffgw_reference/emri_peters_flux.py`. Current in-carrier helpers named `*_reference` for Kerr are useful checks but are **not independent scalar references**.

At the foundation snapshot, the Torch and JAX main dispatchers exposed KerrRingdown and TEOBResumS. TEOBResumS has since been removed from release dispatch; the other four are direct imports from `approximants`. Torch top level exports EMRIKludge and ParameterizedPostEinstein, while JAX top level exports all four. These are API observations, not catalog statements. The current dispatcher enforces a Kerr physical-energy `(2,2,0)` scope that is narrower than direct class calls.

For Kerr, compare native QNM interpolation and spherical/spheroidal projections to independently evaluated radial-angular solutions and scalar harmonics. Its FD poles must be checked against a quadrature of the *real* causal TD polarization, including both poles and the `t0` factor. For TEOB, split Torch's vacuum conditioned EOB mathematics from the shared fallback prototype before any shared model identity; JAX `physical_event` uses a grid maximum while Torch refines a bracketed maximum. For CW, use an SI amplitude scalar and a separate barycentric detector geometry; do not adopt the circular-orbit shortcut as source truth. For memory, implement Talbot's angular kernel directly from spherical-harmonic quadrature, then add oscillatory strain. For EMRI, choose and implement a published AK or NK branch from primary equations. For ppE, implement a paper-literal chirp-mass variable and cosmological `D0` separately from a carrier-convention adapter.

## Evidence and limitations

The CW amplitude slice has a scalar-to-Torch/JAX CPU float64 value and frequency-derivative test, plus a class-wrapper inertia propagation check. On `len`, 32 focused CW reference/parity cases and the four existing CW tests passed with Torch 2.14.0+cpu, JAX 0.11.2 and mpmath 1.3.0. Three nonzero amplitude/derivative holdouts use relative tolerance `1e-13`; JKS v1 PDF Eq. (25)'s rounded 100-Hz rotation benchmark gives `4.22855608095e-25` against its quoted `4.23e-25`. This is a **quadrupole amplitude component** claim only.

The phase/response scalar checks the paper's ideal beam geometry, the monochromatic `2f` phase and frequency gradient after explicitly setting the native circular orbit phase to zero at `t0`, and the `2f` projection conditioned on native phase and antenna inputs. An independent JKS PDF Eqs. (1)–(6) arm-tensor calculation agrees with the closed beam formula of Eqs. (10)–(13) for three JKS site-angle/sky cases. For H1 it checks that both frontends' ECEF arms round to the official four-decimal arm vectors in [Anderson et al. 2001, Appendix B Table I](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0008066v1), and agrees with native Torch/JAX beam values to absolute `2e-15` at three sky/time/polarization holdouts per frontend, conditioned on each frontend's GMST. This is a **required green detector-algebra gate** at the source's printed geometry precision; it does not independently verify GMST or sub-print survey digits. On `len` with Torch 2.14.0+cpu, JAX 0.11.2, mpmath 1.3.0, and float64 CPU inputs, the scoped CW suite had **49 passed, 254 deselected and 3 strict expected failures**.

The [dominant-22 memory component](memory-dominant-22.md) independently
projects Favata's full angular pattern onto `h20`, then checks explicit-SI
Torch/JAX values and amplitude/inclination derivatives at four inclinations
on a nonuniform grid. On `len` with CPU float64, Torch 2.14.0+cpu, JAX 0.11.2,
and mpmath 1.3.0, the initial combined focused run returned **5 passed, 1 strict expected
failure** at relative `5e-13` (absolute strain `1e-35`). The separately selected
strict expected failure is the old `1/√6` coefficient compared with the published symmetric
`±22` coefficient. This is a scoped component gate, not general memory
coupling or qualification of the polarization-only class call.

Three strict expected failures remain as source-variant diagnostics. At `dt=43200 s`, `f1=2×10⁻¹⁰ Hz/s`, `f2=-10⁻¹⁵ Hz/s²`, and `τ=219.0772992826719 s`, the carrier's extra terms give a `+2.363134163530285×10⁻⁵ rad` residual against the paper-truncated phase. At GPS `1126259462`, `ra=1.1`, `dec=-0.3`, `psi=0.27`, native H1 `F+`, `F×` differ from historical JKS Table 1 by `-0.00753319`, `-0.00306511` when both use native GMST; the Table 1 diagnostic fails in both frontends at absolute `5×10⁻⁴`. JKS Table 1 cites Allen 1996 for its site angles; Anderson Appendix B explicitly corrects the LIGO arm orientations in Allen and prints the vectors used by the native model. Thus those two historical-site failures are not antenna-algebra failures for the corrected vectors. PDF Appendix A Eq. (125) uses an SSB-arrival-time polynomial; Eqs. (130)–(131) truncate its delay expansion under the paper's quarter-cycle criterion and Eq. (14) is the corresponding first-order form. Native uses a simplified untruncated polynomial in `dt+τ`. Its additional `τ²` and `τ³` terms should not be removed merely to match the truncated paper formula.

The paper's `t=0` is an SSB reference epoch; its orbital `φ_o` and local sidereal `φ_r` must be supplied. Native `t0` instead zeros orbital phase at that GPS epoch, and native GMST approximates UT1 by UTC. Native `detector=None` also uses H1 antenna patterns while omitting H1's diurnal phase delay. A physical GPS-to-SSB epoch and ephemeris test remains open. The [Kerr 220 carrier](kerr-ringdown.md), [angular projection](kerr-angular-projection.md), and [selected QNM input](kerr-qnm-scalar-qualification.md), and [leading EMRI Peters flux](emri-kludge.md) specifications record their separate scalar checks. The other rows are static research audit unless separate evidence says otherwise. No component here is promoted into the public `WaveformDefinition` catalog.

Source conventions and unresolved limits before qualification:

1. In the authoritative [JKS gr-qc/9804014v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9804014v1), detector response is Eqs. (1)–(6) and (10)–(13), first-order phase is Eq. (14), geometric delay Eq. (18), the two signal harmonics Eqs. (19)–(22), CW amplitude **Eq. (23)**, ellipticity Eq. (24), and the numerical example Eq. (25). The arXiv HTML conversion shifts equation numbering; use the v1 PDF numbers. Using GW frequency requires `f0=2f_rot`; the `f_rot` free-precession branch is outside this principal-axis carrier. JKS Appendix A Eqs. (125), (130)–(131) explain why Eq. (14) omits higher delay powers. The native polynomial in `dt+τ` adds `2π[f1 τ²/2+f2 dt τ²/2+f2 τ³/6]`, so exact Eq. (14) identity remains a failed diagnostic. JKS Table 1 cites [Allen 1996](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9607075) as [25], while [Anderson et al. 2001 Appendix B Table I](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0008066v1) explicitly corrects its LIGO arm orientations; native H1 arms match the latter at four-decimal precision, and independent arm-tensor response passes. The historical JKS Table 1 diagnostic remains failed without altering detector geometry.
2. The legacy memory carrier's `1/√6` sum is not Talbot's mode-pair `Γ` contraction. Favata Eq. (21)'s dominant-mode angular pattern is `sin²i(17+cos²i)`, rather than the carrier's lone `sin²i`. Its distance inference also changes units based on sample magnitude. The separately named `evaluate_22` slice fixes these issues only for a supplied symmetric `±22` pair with explicit distance; it does not accept a polarization-only input as a published memory source.
3. The [EMRI provisional specification](emri-kludge.md) derives `p=A(1-e²)` from Peters 1964 Eqs. (5.6)–(5.7); the resulting `de/dt` coefficient is `304/15`, correcting the former `604/48` in both carriers. This qualifies only leading secular flux. The frequency helper's `e` has no effect, and initial-rate linear evolution is neither Barack–Cutler AK nor Babak et al. NK. The former carrier citation to arXiv:2008.06071 had the wrong title and is not a mathematical source for this implementation.
4. The ppE helper cites Yunes–Pretorius Eq. (1) while replacing its chirp-mass `u=π𝓜 f` with total-mass velocity and omitting amplitude and merger/ringdown branches. The dispersion helper's local-distance substitute for cosmological `D0`, extra apparent redshift factor and π² coefficient do not visibly follow Mirshekari Eqs. (14), (28)–(29). Its docstrings also misstate both paper titles. These are **unresolved mismatches**, not proposed corrections.
5. TEOB's prototype `h22`, fixed horizon, taper and FFT do not establish Nagar et al.'s calibrated multimode/NQC/postmerger waveform. A separately checked natural cubic spline peak finder alone does not qualify the full composition.

The next independent evidence should record exact variant, parameter/domain range, float precision and device, value and derivative metrics, tolerance, negative/failed examples, and source/asset hashes. Compare Torch and JAX independently to that scalar source, then to each other; keep same-model external comparison and physical validation separate. Continue Kerr beyond the selected 220 QNM and angular inputs to other modes, overtones, and excitation physics; the other five need source-convention and scalar-reference gates before a waveform-level claim.
